01 / OUR MISSION

Make RSI
benefit everyone.

Progress builds on generations of shared ideas, code, data, and experience. Its next chapter should be open to everyone.

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) explores how AI can help improve AI itself. We aim to keep that process open through shared platforms and tools, so researchers, independent labs, and domain experts can participate—and build on one another’s discoveries.
